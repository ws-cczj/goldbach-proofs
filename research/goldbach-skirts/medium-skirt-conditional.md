# Conditional proof of the medium-modulus Goldbach skirt

[GoldbachMediumSkirtConditional.lean](./GoldbachMediumSkirtConditional.lean) proves `Goldbach.GRH → (the frozen statement of L-goldbach-frontier-skirt-medium-moduli)` in the declaration `SkirtDiagnostic`. The file compiled standalone with Lean 4.36.0 and Mathlib `fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842`. Lean reported only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The GRH premise remains explicit, and the original task has no authority verdict from this draft.

The proof has two parts. The signed principal model has a lower bound of `-εn` independently of GRH. The relevant declarations are `GoldbachGRHSignedModel.harmonic_suffix_floor` and `GoldbachMediumModulusSkirt.model_floor`. The second part transfers this bound to the actual prime exponential sum. `GoldbachMediumModulusSkirt.estimation_error` uses `GoldbachGRHApproximation.eventual_cell_l2`, which assumes Dirichlet GRH.

For an unconditional proof, let `V(n)` be the squared discrepancy between the prime exponential sum and the principal model, integrated over the medium skirt. The sharp Parseval and Chebyshev energy estimate gives a transfer bound of order `sqrt(n log(n) V(n)) + V(n)`. Thus `V(n) = o(n/log(n))` suffices if the sharper energy estimate is used. A direct lower bound on the signed transfer error is another route. The outstanding estimate is uniform at the highest moduli, of order `n^(1/3)`.
